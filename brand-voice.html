🏆 Tips for a great brand voice
  • 🎯Be specific: "Fun and punchy for fitness enthusiasts aged 20–35" beats "friendly."
  • 📐Set length rules: "Keep captions under 120 characters" or "always write 3 short paragraphs."
  • 😊Emoji guidance: Tell it how many to use and which style fits your brand.
  • 🚫Say what to avoid: "No corporate buzzwords", "never use exclamation points", "avoid hashtags in the body."
  • 📣Add a CTA style: "Always end with a question", "include a soft CTA", or "no CTA."
  • 🔄Update often: Your brand voice evolves — revisit this quarterly.
