Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SnapCaption AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Photos you upload: Images are sent to our AI model to generate captions. We do not store your photos on our servers after processing is complete.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password.
  • Usage data: We log how many captions you've generated per day for rate-limiting purposes.
  • Caption history: For Premium users, we store your previously generated captions so you can review them in your dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate captions and hashtags from your photos using AI
  • To enforce daily usage limits for free-tier users
  • To provide account features such as caption history and brand voice
  • To send transactional emails (password resets, receipts) — never marketing unless you opt in
  • To improve our service through aggregate, anonymised analytics

3. Your Photos

Photos you upload are transmitted securely over HTTPS and passed to OpenAI's API for caption generation. We instruct OpenAI not to train on your data. We do not permanently store your original photos on our servers. Processed thumbnails used for caption history are deleted when you delete your account.

4. Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: Caption generation. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: Payment processing. We never store your card details. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google / Apple: Optional sign-in only. We receive your name and email; we do not access any other data from these accounts.

5. Data Retention

Free-tier usage logs are retained for 30 days. Premium caption history is retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@snapcaptioner.com.

7. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. No cookie banners needed — we keep it simple.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. We regularly review and update our security practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify registered users by email for any significant changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@snapcaptioner.com or visit our Contact page.
