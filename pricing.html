❓ FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
+
Nope. The Free plan is free forever — just open the app and start uploading photos. No account required, no card, no trial countdown.
What's a "caption set"?
+
One caption set = uploading one photo and generating captions for it. Each set gives you 5 unique captions, 20 hashtags, a hook score, alt text, and best time to post. Free users get 3 sets per day. Premium is unlimited.
Can I cancel anytime?
+
Yes, completely. Cancel from your account dashboard anytime — your Premium access continues through the end of your billing period, then you drop back to Free automatically. No cancellation fees.
How does regional pricing work?
+
When you open the checkout, we detect your country and automatically apply the local discount. No code needed. If you're in India, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America, or other PPP regions, you'll see a lower price automatically on the payment screen.
What's the AI photo enhancement?
+
Premium users can click "Enhance Photo" to run your image through GPT Image 1 — it boosts colors, lighting, and sharpness automatically. Free users see a blurred before/after preview so you know what you're missing. Premium includes 30 enhancements per month; Teams gets unlimited.
Can I upgrade or downgrade mid-month?
+
Yes. Upgrading takes effect immediately. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period. All your saved captions and brand voice settings are preserved even if you downgrade.
Does the Teams plan support more than 5 people?
+
The standard Teams plan includes 5 seats. Need more? Contact us at hello@snapcaption.ai and we'll set up a custom plan for larger teams and agencies.
